To stop being online, try the following:

Windows

  1. Press the Windows key on your keyboard.
  2. Type "Powershell" and press Enter to open Powershell.
  3. Paste the following: 
    Start-Process powershell -ArgumentList '-Command "Disable-NetAdapter -Name * -Confirm:$false"' -Verb RunAs
  4. Press Enter. You will be asked for your password.

macOS

  1. Press CMD-space on your keyboard.
  2. Type "Terminal" and press Enter to open Terminal.
  3. Paste the following: 
    for i in $(ifconfig -l); do sudo ifconfig "$i" down 2>/dev/null; done
  4. Press Enter. You will be asked for your password.

Linux

  1. Open a terminal emulator.
  2. Paste the following: 
    sudo ip route del default ; sudo ip -6 route del default
  3. Press Enter. You may be asked for your password.

iOS

  1. Go to Settings.
  2. In the search bar, type "Airplane Mode". Tap the first result, which will be "Airplane Mode".
  3. Toggle Airplane Mode to on.

Android

  1. Swipe down from the top of the screen to reveal Quick Settings.
  2. Look for the airplane icon and tap it.
