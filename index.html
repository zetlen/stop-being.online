To stop being online, try the following:

Windows

  1. Press the Windows key on your keyboard.
  2. Type "Powershell" and press Enter to open Powershell.
  3. Paste the following: 
    Start-Process powershell -ArgumentList '-Command "Disable-NetAdapter -Name * -Confirm:$false"' -Verb RunAs
  4. Press Enter. You will be asked for your password.

macOS

  1. Press CMD-space on your keyboard.
  2. Type "Terminal" and press Enter to open Terminal.
  3. Paste the following: 
    for i in $(ifconfig -l); do sudo ifconfig "$i" down 2>/dev/null; done
  4. Press Enter. You will be asked for your password.

Linux

  1. Open a terminal emulator.
  2. Paste the following: 
    sudo ip route del default ; sudo ip -6 route del default
  3. Press Enter. You may be asked for your password.

iOS

  1. Go to the Settings app.
  2. In the search bar, type "Screen Time". Tap the first result, which will be "Screen Time".
  3. If you don't have Screen Time active, you will see "App & Website Activity". Tap that menu, click "Turn on App & Website Activity".
  4. Scroll to "Lock Screen Time Settings". Tap it.
  5. Find a friend. Hand your phone to the friend. Tell them you would like to stop being online, and that they should make up a four-digit passcode and not tell you what it is. They will need to enter it twice. It will ask for a backup Apple Account. Skip this.
  6. Have your friend stick around for the next couple of steps to enter the passcode every time it asks.
  7. Tap "Downtime".
  8. Turn on "Scheduled". Set "Every Day".
  9. In the Time menu, set 12:00AM-11:59PM.
  10. Toggle "Block at Downtime" to on.
  11. Go back. Scroll to and tap "Lock Screen Time Settings".
  12. Thank your friend. Reboot your phone.

Android

  1. Go to the Settings app.
  2. Tap "Network & internet".
  3. Turn off Wi-Fi. Tap your SIM card and turn off "Mobile data".
  4. Go back to "Network & internet". Tap "Private DNS". Select "Private DNS provider hostname".
  5. Find a friend. Hand your phone to the friend. Tell them you would like to stop being online, and that they should type a long, random hostname and not tell you what it is. Tap "Save".
  6. Have your friend stick around for the next few steps.
  7. Go back to Settings. Tap "Apps". Find each web browser you have installed (Chrome, Firefox, etc.). Tap it, then tap "Disable" and confirm. Repeat for each browser.
  8. Find "Google Play Store" in the app list. Tap "Disable" and confirm.
  9. Go back to Settings. Tap "Digital Wellbeing & parental controls". Tap "Focus mode". Select every app in the list. Set a schedule for every day, all day.
  10. Thank your friend. Reboot your phone.
